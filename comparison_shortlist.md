# Comparison shortlist

Shortened from `comparison_draft.md`, which compares `assignment2_baseline.json` with `ai_requirements.json`. **R**, **S**, and **B** identify baseline requirements, user stories, and backlog items; FR, NFR, US, and PB identify LLM items. Differences are relative to the baseline artifact. Related items do not establish that a missing feature is specified, and open questions do not supply requirements. Repeated differences across sections are consolidated below. Decisions and justifications are left for manual completion.
