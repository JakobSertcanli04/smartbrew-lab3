# Semantic comparison draft

Sources: `assignment2_baseline.json` (the available Assignment 2 baseline; no `baseline.json` is present) and `ai_requirements.json`. This compares the two artifacts, without filling either artifact's gaps from `smartbrew-spec`.

Baseline references: **R** = `requirements`, **S** = `user_stories`, **B** = `product_backlog`. LLM references use their stored FR, NFR, US, and PB IDs. Labels in the Difference column describe the LLM relative to the baseline. An addition is new relative to the baseline artifact, not necessarily new to the system description. Separate rows distinguish added and omitted facets of the same capability. Related items cited beside a missing facet do not establish that facet's presence.

The baseline has no dedicated non-functional section; embedded constraints are considered below. LLM assumptions and open questions identify unresolved matters and are not treated as completed requirements. Decisions and justifications are reserved for manual completion.
